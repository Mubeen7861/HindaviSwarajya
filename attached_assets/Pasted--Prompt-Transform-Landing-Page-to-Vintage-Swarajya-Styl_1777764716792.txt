## 🎨 Prompt — Transform Landing Page to Vintage Swarajya Style

Redesign the existing landing page UI into a **vintage, raw, 17th-century Hindavi Swarajya-inspired style**, influenced by the era of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

---

### 🎯 Goal:

Convert the current modern UI into a **historic, cinematic, and powerful experience** while keeping usability intact.

---

## 🎨 Style Transformation Rules

### 1. Background

* Replace clean/flat backgrounds with:

  * Old parchment texture
  * Aged paper / stone texture
  * Subtle grain, dust, scratches
* Use warm earthy tones:

  * Burnt saffron
  * Mud brown
  * Dark charcoal

---

### 2. Color System

* Primary: Deep saffron (muted, not bright)
* Secondary: Dark brown / near black
* Accent: Antique gold (very subtle highlights)
* Remove:

  * Bright gradients
  * Neon colors
  * Modern blue/purple palettes

---

### 3. Typography

* Headings:

  * Bold serif or Devanagari-style font
  * Slight rough / carved feel
* Body:

  * Keep clean and readable (modern for usability)

---

### 4. UI Components

#### Buttons:

* Style like **stamped metal or carved wood**
* Slight rough edges
* Press effect (like a seal/stamp)

#### Cards / Sections:

* Look like **aged paper blocks**
* Soft shadows, not glassy

#### Icons:

* Replace modern icons with:

  * Minimal engraved-style icons
  * Thin line + rough edges

---

### 5. Decorative Elements (Subtle Only)

* Add faded:

  * Fort silhouettes
  * Sword textures
  * Bhagwa flag hints
* Keep opacity low (background only, not distracting)

---

### 6. Motion & Effects

* Add:

  * Slow dust particles
  * Light rays
  * Soft glow on hover
* Avoid:

  * Fast animations
  * Tech-style transitions

---

### 7. Badge Integration

* Use coin badges as:

  * Highlights
  * Achievements
* Add slight metallic shine (subtle)

---

### 8. Overall Tone

* Raw
* Historic
* Purpose-driven
* Feels like a **movement, not a startup**

---

## ⚡ Balance Rule (IMPORTANT)

Maintain:

* 60% usability
* 40% vintage styling

Do NOT:

* Reduce readability
* Over-texture the UI
* Make it feel outdated or cluttered

---

## 🧠 Final Feel

The interface should feel like:
👉 “A digital Swarajya platform built in today’s world”

Not:
❌ A history-themed website
❌ A modern SaaS product

---

### 🔥 One-line Direction

👉 “Turn this UI into a cinematic, raw Swarajya-era experience while keeping it clean and usable.”
