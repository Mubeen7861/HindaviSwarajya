## ⚔️ How It Works — Hindavi Swarajya

Create a clean, minimal, and powerful **“How It Works” section** for a platform inspired by the philosophy of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

### 🎯 Goal:

Explain the system in **4–5 simple steps** so that any user instantly understands:
👉 “What should I do?”
👉 “What will I get?”

Tone should be:

* Simple
* Motivational
* Action-driven
* Slight Marathi cultural touch

---

## 🪶 Section Title Options:

* “How It Works”
* “स्वराज्य कसे घडते”
* “Your Journey in Swarajya”

---

## ⚡ Steps (Final Content)

### 1️⃣ Do Seva

**“Start with action.”**
Help someone in real life — food, education, health, or any meaningful support.

---

### 2️⃣ Share Your Impact

**“Inspire others.”**
Post your seva on Instagram and tag us to spread the movement.

---

### 3️⃣ Submit Proof

**“Make it count.”**
Paste your post link on our platform and submit your contribution.

---

### 4️⃣ Get Verified

**“Earn trust.”**
Our team reviews your submission to ensure genuine impact.

---

### 5️⃣ Rise in Swarajya

**“Earn respect, not just points.”**
Gain points, unlock ranks like Mavla, Sardar, and become a part of something bigger.

---

## 🔥 Closing Line (Important)

👉
**“This is not just a platform.
This is a movement built on seva.”**

or

👉
**“हे फक्त प्लॅटफॉर्म नाही… हे स्वराज्य आहे.”**

---

## 🎨 UI Suggestions (for your design)

* Use **5 horizontal cards or steps**

* Each step with:

  * Icon (Seva / Share / Link / Verify / Rank)
  * Short bold title
  * 1-line description

* Add subtle **arrow flow → → →**

* Background: Light with saffron accent

---

## ⚡ Micro Interaction (Optional but 🔥)

* On hover:

  * Step highlights
  * Small animation (arrow moving forward)

---

## 🧠 Final Feeling

User should feel:
👉 “This is simple”
👉 “This is meaningful”
👉 “I want to start now”

Avoid:
❌ Long paragraphs
❌ Complex explanation
❌ Corporate tone

Keep it **sharp, emotional, and clear**
